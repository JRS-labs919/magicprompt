# Variant A — Document / spec / RFQ review or extraction

Add to the template:

```
FINDINGS STRUCTURE
1. Blocking issues — numbered, each with source citation and why it blocks
2. Internal inconsistencies — contradictions inside a single document
3. VERIFY externally — anything not confirmable from the documents

PRODUCT AND MODEL NAMES
Any product name, model number, or part number that cannot be confirmed
present in the sources goes in (3) labeled VERIFY. Do not assert that a
product exists, is current, or has a given capability from recall.
Tag findings per the claim-tag rules; an unconfirmed name is [U].

SEQUENCING
No recommendations until the gaps are listed. Gaps first, opinion second.
```

**Why:** in reviews, the confidently-stated product name is the most common fabrication, and it's the hardest to spot because it sits among correct ones. Forcing every unconfirmable name into a labeled bucket makes the distinction structural rather than a matter of tone.

**Common inconsistency checks worth compiling in:** quantities in the schedule versus the narrative; a device number assigned twice; ratings that drift between the spec basis and the drawings; jurisdictional boilerplate recycled from another project; duplicate or misfiled documents (verify integrity, don't assume); count reconciliation between feeder positions and named loads.
